IT training in Abuja for the real world.

Build practical technology and communication skills with Yazz Communication Academy. Our programmes are designed for learners at different stages, from school-age students and beginners to graduates, NYSC members, professionals and organizations.

Digital skills and technology training

Yazz Communication Academy provides practical computer training, programming, data analysis, web development, software engineering, cybersecurity, design and animation education. Training can be delivered for individuals, schools, teams and organizations in Abuja and across the Federal Capital Territory.

AI, data and modern technology

Our digital-skills direction includes modern technology topics such as artificial intelligence, productivity with digital tools, Python, data analysis and technology workflows. Contact the academy for the current AI and digital-skills schedule and available cohorts.

Training for students, graduates and professionals

Learning pathways can be tailored for primary and secondary learners, undergraduates, interns, NYSC members and graduates, as well as working professionals who need practical technology skills. Younger learners can begin with age-appropriate computer and visual programming activities.

Yazz Communication Academy Abuja campus

Our Abuja location is Plot 1 Yazz Avenue, Off El-Habitat Close, Behind Marigold Hotel, Dogongada, Abuja. Yazz Communication Academy serves learners and organizations across Abuja and the FCT, including Lokogoma, Kabusa and Damagaza, with training options shaped around the learner's location, level and goals. We also support online learning where appropriate.

FAQ / YCA

Questions learners ask.

Useful answers help both people and search engines understand what Yazz Communication Academy offers.

Where can I learn IT skills in Abuja?

Yazz Communication Academy provides practical IT and digital-skills training for learners in Abuja and across the FCT. Programmes include computer literacy, data analysis with Python, web development, software engineering, cybersecurity, programming, design and corporate training.

Where can I learn cybersecurity in Abuja?

Yazz Communication Academy offers a practical cybersecurity programme covering security fundamentals, networking, vulnerabilities, ethical hacking foundations and defensive practice.

Can I learn data analysis with Python in Abuja?

Yes. The Data Analysis with Python programme covers Python foundations, NumPy, Pandas, data cleaning, exploratory analysis, visualisation and practical projects.

Do you train students and young learners?

Yes. Learning can be adapted for school-age learners, undergraduates, interns, NYSC members, graduates and professionals. Younger learners can start with age-appropriate computer and visual programming activities.

Do you provide corporate IT training?

Yes. Corporate training can be tailored around digital productivity, software workflows, communication, collaboration and role-specific technology skills.

Do you offer communication and public speaking training?

Communication-focused training can be tailored for individuals, students, professionals and organizations. Contact the academy for the current communication and public-speaking programme schedule.
